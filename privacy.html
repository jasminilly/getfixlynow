3. SMS and MMS Messaging

Fixly provides conversational support through text and multimedia messages. When you enroll in or otherwise consent to an ongoing Fixly messaging program, message frequency varies based on your interactions with Fixly and the support you request. Message and data rates may apply. Consent to receive messages is not a condition of purchasing any goods or services.

You may withdraw consent to recurring SMS/MMS communications at any time by replying STOP. You may reply HELP for assistance. After an opt-out request, we may send a final confirmation message and will stop further messages covered by that consent unless you subsequently opt in again. Your wireless carrier is not responsible for delayed or undelivered messages.

We maintain messaging consent and opt-out information as reasonably necessary to honor communication preferences, document consent, comply with telecommunications requirements, and prevent messages from being sent after an applicable opt-out.

No mobile information will be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes.
